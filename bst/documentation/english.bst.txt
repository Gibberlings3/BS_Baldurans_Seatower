*Citizens of Baldur's Gate!

Come and take a stroll around the Balduran's Seatower!

Enter and be at awe at the great architecture, the wonders, and hidden treasures that will await anyone bold enough to enter the tower with adventures in mind!*	

BS Balduran's Seatower Modification (BST) is a mod for BGT, BG:EE, and EET, adding a new part to Baldur's Gate City, with several larger and small quests and encounters inside the new city district, outside in new dungeons, as well as related additions to the existing areas.

v1 is released as a public beta. Keep in mind that there could still be bugs. Please report anything you notice to the mod's forums!



DESCRIPTION

The Seatower Area is accessible via the far south-west corner of the Docks Disrict. After visiting once, it is accessible via the worldmap.

The mod is directly compatible with BGT, BG:EE w/o SoD, and EET.

If you need the Worldmap mod for other mods, install it after BST.

BST makes changes to the Worldmap, so you need to start a new game if you install the mod.

Have a look at the walkthroughs below if you get stuck in a quest. All quests start by / are available after entering the Seatower area.



COMPONENTS

The first and main component adds the Seatower and all quests to the game.

The second, optional component "Reveal Seatower Map When Entering Area" reveals the main Seatower area upon first entering.

The third, optional component "Adapt Palace Fight with Regard to Doppelganger Invasion" adjusts the fight in the Palace depending on whether the threat was revealed or not.
There is three options:
1. "Place More Flaming Fist soldiers into the Palace if Revealed": this will add one more soldier.
2. "Reduce Number of Doppelgangers in Palace if Revealed": this will remove one doppelganger.
3. "Place More Doppelgangers into Palace if Invasion was not Revealed": this will add one more doppleganger.
All three options should be compatible with Jarl's Adventure Pack's changes to the palace scene.

The fourth component is for BGT only: "Correct music entries for BGT". It will correct the area music of BST areas in BGT to match fitting ones, otherwise some area music would be out of place.
It is not needed for BGT Music choice "Hybrid BG1 and BGII Music". This is the default case considered. In case "Hybrid BG1 and BGII Music" component is installed OR the game is not BGT, this component will be skipped automatically.



LIST OF QUESTS


"A Girl inside the Sarcophagus":
-------------------------------
Aquired by opening the sarcophagus in the crypt area of the lower inner temple ruins.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island.

Walkthrough:
- Open the Sarcophagus and talk to A Girl.
- Open the Sarcophagus again. Fight the Doomsayer "Binky".
- A Girl will talk to you again and there will be a small cutscene when she goes away.


"A Hammer for the Pirates":
-------------------------------
Given by the Pirate Capatin on the outside of the Old Temple Island.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island.

Walkthrough:
- Talk to the Pirate Captain. He will mention that they need a craftman's hammer for the repairs to their ship.
- Talk to the Priest of Valkur in the old chapel on the upper inner level of the temple ruins.
- Give the Priest of Valkur either a war hammer, a mace, or a morning star in exchange for the craftman's hammer. The hammer will also drop if the priest dies.
- Bring the craftman's hammer to the Pirate Captain.
Note: an appropriate weapon can either be bought from the castaway headmaster (quest "The Door Riddle"), the pirate Dirk outside, or found in a hidden stash in the cave system (Note: to find the hidden stash, infravision is required).


"Another Smuggler Errand":
-------------------------------
Given by Holger if the smuggler errands in BG1 were finished ("Working with the Smugglers").
This will be another smuggler errand which will either trigger after Sarevok's death (for BGT / with a mod that keeps the game in BG1 after S. death), or SoD.

-Talk to Holger. He is in the Three Old Kegs ground floor (same spot for BG1 / SoD).
-Take the box to Bruce, a Flaming Fist soldier inside the palace, 2nd floor.
-Talk to Holger again to get your reward.
Note: For BG:SoD/EET with e.g. EndlessBG1, the quest can also be started in BG1 and finished in SoD.


"An Orphan in the Seatower": 
-------------------------------
Given after talking to the "Legal Assistant" on the first floor of the Seatower (Inn). 

Walkthrough: PC needs to talk to Lady Springfall on the second floor of the tower and tell her how the boy looks. PC can lie for evil quest end. Quest ends after meeting the boy on the first floor again.
There are two quest ends:
-if the PC lies to Lady Springfall she will abandon the boy.
-if the PC tells the truth Lady Springfall will adopt the boy.
Quest reward item is "Lars' Drawing" which has no use whatsoever. 


"Bandit Communication":
-------------------------------
Given by the bandit in the Seatower Prison. He is in the second to right cell in the first aisle.
Prerequisite: only evil aligned PC; quest "The Prisoner in the Seatower Prison" is aquired/finished.
Note: the prison cell doors will be locked each time the prison was left. The prison key is required to open them again. Do not lose this key, there will be no replacement.

Walkthrough: Talk to the bandit. When the dialogue is about to end, the bandit will ask the PC to deliver a note to a contact.
- Accept the note and place it into the hiding place specififed. There will be 500 gp inside already. Upon returning to the hiding place (going away far enough is sufficient to trigger the change), the note will be gone and there will be another 500gp.
- After the note was taken from his contacts, the bandit will be gone from the Seatower Prison. Talk to the Tower Guard Jay to get a scolding, including one loss in reputation.
The note is written in code so it can't be read. The hiding place is on a tree on Wyrm's Crossing, on the other side of the river than the city (side of Tenya's house), on a single tree with read leaves (around [4330.1992]).
Note: for the EEs, the hiding place container will indeed be hidden until the bandit talks about it. For BGT, it will be there but empty before talking to the bandit.


"Betrayed by the Priestess of Umberlee?":
-------------------------------
Aquired by finding evidence on the temple island or afterwards.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is either on the Old Temple Island or back after finishing it.

Walkthrough:
- In the cave system on the island, collect the note on one of the dead adventurers.
- Talk to the Sahuagin Priestess on the island and ask about the mentioned conspiracy.
- The main Sahuagin baron on the island also tells about the conspiracy.
- After returning to the Sea Cave under the Balduran's Seatower, confront the Priestessess of Umberlee with the accusations or just attack them.
- Collect the compromising letter from one of the dead priestesses.
There is two options:
1. Bring the letter to the Seatower Commander.
2. Bring the evidence to either Jalantha or Tenya (inside the tmple), if they are still available. Note: they will also request the tooth relic and attack if the PC can't provide it.

Note: the quest can also be aquired if no hints were found on the island but only the evidence was collected after confronting the Priestesses of Umberlee.


"Block the Malenti Water Hole":
-------------------------------
Given by the quartermaster of the castaways in the hidden rooms on the upper inner level of the temple ruins.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island. Quest "The Door Riddle" needs to be finished to gain access to the hidden rooms.

Walkthrough:
- Ask to quartermaster about how much water they'd need.
- Place a boulder into the water hole the Malenti are coming through. Boulders can be found on the outside area of the temple island.
- Tell quartermaster that the hole is blocked.


"Cage the Rabbits":
-------------------------------
Given by Inn Master Roryn after incident on third floor for quest "Strange Happenings in Room No. 3.4" if none of the rabbits were killed.

Walkthrough: The PC receives a cage and has to collect the rabbits on the third level of the Seatower (done by klicking on the rabbits and picking them up via dialogue). Quest ends after giving the cage back to Inn Master Roryn.
There is 11 rabbits in total. The quest can be ended any time by giving back the cage, quest reward is 50 gold for every caught rabbit. 


"Candle for the Redeemed":
-------------------------------
Given by the man formerly being a Higher Lacedon (quest "Umberlee's Curse").
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island, and "Umberlee's Curse" is finished.

Walkthrough:
- Collect the candle from the altar in the crypt area of the lower inner temple ruins.
- Bring the candle to the quest giver.


"Confront the Slave Traders in Baldur's Gate": 
-------------------------------
Given by Seatower Commander. 

Walkthrough: After learning that Leonhard "the Fierce" on the third floor of the Seatower is a slaver contact, the PC can tell the Seatower Commander about it. To confront the slave traders two things are needed:
1. the PC needs to gather information about *where* the slavers will transfer slaves next.
2. the PC needs to gather information about *when* the slavers will transfer slaves next.
There are different possibilities to gather these information, depending on whether the PC is working with the smugglers or not (quest "Working with the Smugglers"):
-If working with the smugglers, the PC will be summoned to Leonhard "the Fierce" into the Seatower and get information about when and where. With these information, the PC can go to the Seatower Commander.
-Independent on whether the PC is working with the smugglers, the information can be gathered by freeing Shuck's brother (quest "Help Shuck's Brother") and telling the Seatower Commander about it, and killing Leonhard "the Fierce" which will drop a booklet with all the dates. In this case the knowledge about where will be aquired via Shuck's freed brother by the Seatower Commander (PC has to leave and return to the Commander for him to have the information).
3. After time and place are known, the slave traders can be confronted at night on the map with the bridge (Wyrm's Crossing) in the far south corner on the west bank of the river. A Flaming Fist Soldier will approach the PC before the meeting place of the slavers.
4. The PC needs to fight the slavers at the meeting point.
5. The quest is finished after talking to the Seatower Commander again.
Note: in BG:EE/EET the area can be left at the west border. Do not leave the area during the fight with the slavers because returning via the worldmap will bring you to the bridge again and not into the south corner so you'll have to walk back to the meeting point which might be immersion breaking.
Note: If the quest to save Shuck's brother was declined and the PC does not work with the smugglers, the slavers cannot be confronted. The booklet can still be brought to the Seatower Commander in case Leonhard was killed.
Note: if the PC works with the smugglers (quest "Working with the Smugglers"), the slavers can be confronted without the smugglers turning hostile as long as Leonhard "the Fierce", the slaver contact, was not killed.
Note: If the PC does not work with the slavers but with the smugglers, confrontation of the slavers and especially Leonhard the Fierce will happen by itself and the notebook can then be handed to the commander without risking the smuggler's cooperation.


"Dark Power in the Seatower Catacombs": 
-------------------------------
Given by abbot in the Ilmater Monastery. You can ask for tasks the first time you approach him.

Note: the keys disappear after use, including the key to the inner catacombs.
Note: This quest was revised and extended since the Early Access versions. It always contains fighting Undead now.

Walkthrough:
- Enter catacombs through the mausoleum in Chionthar Tower (southwest).
- Clear the outer area of the catacombs from undead.
- Find the key that was dropped in the catacombs. [Possible quest ending: give now key back to the abbot.]
- Open hidden door to the inner catacombs. Talk to monk Fuller.
- Clear the inner catacombs from undead.
- Enter the inner sanctum and talk to the King of the Restless Dead.
- Collect dropped spell scroll. [Possible quest ending: give scroll to Fuller.]
- Talk to Fuller or not, tell him to leave or not. Note: if Fuller stays, he'll most probably die in the coming fight with undead.
- Do the banning ritual using the scroll. [complete quest ending].
In all cases, collect your reward from the abbot.


"Defense System of the "Arena" Room":
-------------------------------
Aquired by entering the guarded "Arena" room.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island.

Walkthrough:
- Enter the "Arena" room either by the main entrance (the Sahuagin guards will turn hostile) or by the secret passage (quests "Secret Passage from Chapel to Arena?" and/or "Hidden Mechanism in the Chapel's Altar?").
- The defense system will be activated upon entering the arena without being Invisible.
- Fight the 4 monster waves to deactivate it for good. Number of monsters depends on game difficulty.
1. wave: Skelettons
2. wave: Otyugh
3. wave: Ettercap
4. Wave: Phase Spiders. (for hard/hardest game difficulty also Astral Phase Spider).
- For temporary deactivation, collect the Shell Horn from one of the dead Sahuagin. Blowing it will temporarily deactivate the defense system and kill all currently present monsters. As long as there is still monster waves open, the defense system will reactive upon reentering the arena. The Shell Horn can be used every time. The Shell Horn has no use after all monster waves are done.


"Destroy the Great Portal":
-------------------------------
Given by the Old Hermit in the library on the Old Temple Island.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island. Quest "What is Going On in the Guarded Room?" needs to be finished.
Quest "The Rat in the Library" needs to be finished.

Walkthrough:
- Ask the Old Hermit what can be done to destroy the great portal the Sahuagin are working on.
- Get the Dry Spore Powder from the spore colony in the cave system.
- The Old Hermit will make a special tincture with which the portal can be destroyed.
- Enter the Arena Room and destroy the portal using the tincture. Alternatively, kill all Sahuagin on the island. In this case, the Old Hermit will offer to destroy the portal herself.

Note: There was once a glitch where using the tincture in another area than the arena room let to it being gone. In this case, the quest can't be completed.
Note: If you ask the Old Hermit about Balduran's items, now she will be ready to hand over Balduran's Boots. If you asked about the boots before, she will hand them over now.


"Diddy's Missing Dagger":
-------------------------------
Given by Diddy, a castaway in the hidden rooms on the upper inner level of the temple ruins.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island. Quest "The Door Riddle" needs to be finished to gain access to the hidden rooms.

Walkthrough:
- Talk to Diddy. She will tell about her missing dagger and where she left it.
There is two options to solve the quest.
1. Go into the dormitory adjacent to the hidden rooms and search for Diddy's dagger.
2. Ask Diddy whether she is sure her dagger will be there until the dialogue develops.
- The following dialogue with another Castaway will tell that he has Diddy's dagger now.
1. Tell the other castaway to give Diddy her dagger back.
2. Give Diddy some gold so she can buy a new one from the quartermaster.
3. Do not help Diddy further.
Reward is one healing potion but will only be given if the PC claims it.


"Dispute over Leadership between Castaways":
-------------------------------
Given by castaway prisoner in the hidden room on the lower inner level of the temple ruins.
Prerequisite: quest "Retrieve Iakhovas's Tooth" is aquired and group is on the Old Temple Island. Quest "The Door Riddle" needs to be finished to gain access to the hidden rooms.

Walkthrough:
- Talk to the leader, the prisoner, and the other castaways to learn about the leadership dispute.
If you want to make the castaway held prisoner the new leader:
- Kill the guard that is with him.
- Follow the former prisoner to the hidden rooms on the upper level and watch the scene.
- Collect the former leader's sword as a reward.
If you do not want to switch leadership, free the upper level of the inner ruins from Sahuagin and tell the leader. After that, talk to the now free prisoner.
Note: The quest will also be solved if the guard and/or leader are killed after the Sahuagin threat has been eliminated, but the former prisoner will not take the position any more.


"Doppelgangers Inside the Seatower": 
-------------------------------
This is the main quest inside the tower which is connected to a lot of other quests.
Chapter 1-5: quest starts after or during quest "The Breach in the Seatower Prison": after the hole in the prison wall is reported to the Seatower Commander.
After Sarevok was revealed in the Duchal Palace (or is dead/BGII): quest starts directly by speaking to the Seatower Commander the first time.
Note: after the quest started there will be a quest timer of 5 to one ingame days depending on the quest status. Exceeding the timer while being away from the tower grounds or the seacave will set the quest to completed without the PC's help.

Walkthrough: 
- Quest starts in the Seatower Commander's Office: he will be attacked by two doppelgangers.
- PC gets quest to investigate the hole in the prison wall ("The Breach in the Seatower Prison"). 
-PC has to go through the hole in the prison wall and further until the dopelgangers inside the tailor's shop are killed.
-After reporting about the doppelgangers in the Tailor's Shop to the Seatower Commander (officially for quest "The Breach in the Seatower Prison"), quest will proceed.
- PC will be asked to go into the barracks and help the Captain and the Seatower Mage against the doppelgangers inside the barracks. Refusing will skip to next quest status.
- PC will be asked to help against the doppelgangers on the seatower grounds. Quest item: "Mimic Powder". Approaching or talking to certain soldiers on the tower grounds with quest item in inventory will make them turn into doppelgangers. Refusing will skip to next quest status. Note: Disguised doppelgangers could also be attacked directly. They need to turn into doppelgangers to be counted for the quest, though.
Note: There is a total of five disguised doppelgangers on the tower grounds outside the barracks.
--1 in front of the treasury [1981.1259].
--1 on the balustrade between Dradeel's Tower and Dragon Tower [1532.1971].
--1 on top of the Chionthar Tower [777.1563].
--1 on the little balcony above the main entrance to the Seatower Inn (accessible from inside second floor) [3902.1287].
--1 inside the South Harbor Tower.
- PC can speed up the process by asking the tanner to use the Mimic Powder in one of his tubs to spread the fumes (activates quest "Liqueur for the Tanner" if not declined). If done so, all remaining doppelgangers will gather in front of the baracks. The amount of doppelgangers gathering in front of the barracks will be max 5 dependent on how many were already killed.
Note: if going to the tanner by entering via the Chaintower and going clock wise to the Traitor's Tower, no disguised doppelgangers will be met on the way. If going via South Harbor Tower, 4 of 5 disguised doppelgangers will be met before reaching the tanner.
- PC needs to talk to Captain again after defeating all 5 doppelgangers.
- Quest ends by talking to Seatower Commander.
Note: For BGT and EET, the mimic drops a bottle of Mimic's Blood. This is the BGII item. It can be sold to and rebought from the Seatower mage and serves no purpose in this mod.


"Fire Elemental in the Seatower Storeroom": 
-------------------------------
Given by cook if PC asks about quest item of quest "Halfslinger's Ale". 

Walkthrough: Intermediate quest items: barrel (can be found in cellar), fletcher will coat it with a Potion of Fire Resistance if quest "The Fletcher's Feathers" is finished.
There are five possible quest ends:
- kill the fire elemental in the cellar
- trap the fire elemental in a fire coated barrel and set it free into the tower's beacon flame (on top of the tower)
- trap the fire elemental in a fire coated barrel and drown it in the well (evil quest solution, -1 to reputation)
- trap the fire elemental in a fire coated barrel for more than two ingame days and kill it when it escapes the barrel.
- report the fire elemental to the Captain in the barracks and let the soldiers handle it. The elemental will be gone next the PC goes into the kitchen.

Given by Shuck inside the Seatower Prison.

"Hidden Letters":
-------------------------------
Note: this quest does not have journal entries. It's more of an Easter Egg.

Walkthrough:
- Talk to Yvonne at the entrance to the Seatower area. Afterwards, look into the hidden stash in the wall (very small, around [1120.401]).
1 You can either leave the letter and come back from time to time and see what's inside the hidden stash. If you put all letters back there will eventually a reward.

"Moggy the Cat":
-------------------------------
Note: this quest does not have journal entries. It's more of an Easter Egg.

Walkthrough:
- Pet the cat in the Ilmater Monastery on the Seatower grounds.
Her reactions are completely random, i.e. do not follow a pattern.
She can:
-let you pet her.
-twitches tail
-scratches you
-bites you. Biting will only happen once and will give "Moggy's Wrath" (Disease with CHA -2 malus [I wanted to simulate blood poisoning without making this Easter Egg too severe].)
Note: If you manage to pet her for 6 times in a row without her tail switching (or scratching/biting) she'll purr and it will grant "Moggy's Favor" (+1 Luck bonus for 60 seconds).
Also note Moggy's content for "My Room on the Ilmater Monastery".

"My Room on the Ilmater Monastery":
-------------------------------
After completing the whole quest "Dark Power in the Seatower Catacombs", the PC can use one of the rooms in the monastery. There is a chest and a bed. The bed can be used to rest, or to bide time until certain hours of the day (e.g. midnight etc.).
Note: when biding time, the game time will just be fast forwarded without giving the party resting benefits.
- Monk PCs will get the room for free.
- Non Monk PCs will have to pay 1000gp.

"Working with the Smugglers": 
-------------------------------
Given by Holger (the smuggler) on the Nashkell Carnival south of the Lotus Tent, only after talking to him for the quest "Help Shuck's Brother". 

Note: While the possibility to sell out the smugglers and also, to go about the smuggler quests themselves and how they develop for each path are meant absolutely serious, it is still written with a little tongue-in-cheek. Thus, don't be afraid to role play here - nothing really grave will happen.

Walkthrough:
- PC has to ask to work for Holger. Holger will note the presence of paladins but give the PC a chance nonetheless.
-first quest is to deliver a sealed box to the cook in the Helm and Cloak (timer: three days)
-second quest is to report back about the smugglers in the Seatower. The whereabouts of the smugglers in the Seatower will be known while doing the quest "The Breach in the Seatower Prison".
-third quest is to confront Night Masks. For this, get the crate from Edgar northwest of the Lotus Tent at Nashel Carnival at night and carry it visibly north. Not far away will be Night Masks who will attack. After the Night Masks are dead, there will be soldiers.
Note: chose any non-hostile reply option. The only difference is whether the PC knows what is inside the crate when giving it back to Holger or not. Give the crate back to Holger.
-4th quest is retrieving back a smuggled item from Baldur's Gate, to be precise from the Seatower Barracks, second floor (Captain's quarters). The item can either be stolen. Make sure not to be spotted by the guard on duty. If the PC was caught stealing or for the direct route, the Captain can be talked to about the smuggler item in his possession to give it back. Success will be increased if the PC is not a gnome, has some official document, has helped the Seatower against the doppelgangers inside the barracks at least, and behaves politely.
Note: if the smugglers should be busted ("Reporting Smugglers to the Flaming Fist"), the Nashkel Guard needs to be informed before giving back the fragrant to Holger.
-5th quest: carry a heavy barrel through the smuggler tunnel beneath the Seatower. For this, get the barrel out of the front yard of the tailor shop (Dock's District). Go through the tunnels and the sea cave, emerge to the outside at the coast beneath the Seatower. The smuggler contact will wait here.
The PC has to decide whether to open he barrel or not, and whether to report the smuggler contact or not. Follow-ups will be accordingly.
If the barrel gets opened, Holger will only forgive if the PC did not work for the slavers, or if the PC did all smuggler quests until then.
6th quest: protect Holger in a meeting with a new contact. Just tell Holger when he is ready and wait until the meeting is over.

Note: quests 3-6 can be denied, but it will kill the easter egg follow ups.

Quest cycle in BG1 ends after 6th quest. Smugglers will leave.
After finishing the first two quests for the smugglers, finishing quest "Help Shuck's Brother", and Leonhard "the Fierce" (slave trader contact on the third floor for the tower) still alive, the PC will get a message for quest "Working for the Slavers" after one day if they stated interest in wanting to work for the slavers to Holger.
Note: the smugglers will attack if Leonhard "the Fierce" inside the Seatower was killed too early, i.e. without having a good explanation for Holger. There is quest paths were Leonhard can be killed without repercussions from the smugglers, it will be clear in the game.

There will be a follow-up quest later ("Another Smuggler Errand").

Note: There is reply options tagged with (truth) or (lie). If you answer (truth), journal entries will assume you want to work with the smugglers. if you answer (lie), then it is assumed that you want to play off the smugglers to report them to the soldiers. (It is also assumed that the PC either wants to work for the smugglers and not with the soldiers or vice versa - the detection variable is the same as for working for/against the soldiers, but reversed. Answering inconsistent can lead to wrong journal entries regarding the PC's motivation.)

Easter Eggs:
1: when working / having worked for the smugglers, the smuggler in chapter 6 who offers to take the group inside the city will refer to Holger.
2. for a continuous game in BGT/EET and after having done at least 5 of the 6 smuggler quests in BG1, the smugglers in Amkethran will refer to Holger after the PC helped them to fend off the monks.



INSTALLATION

Note: v1 and higher is not backwards compatible with the eaob versions.

General note: When installing or uninstalling manually, do not close the DOS window by clicking on the X button! Instead, press the Enter key when instructed to do so.


BGII:EE
If you use BG Enhanced Edition from GOG or Steam, you need to prepare your game before installing any mods:
Link: https://forums.beamdog.com/discussion/71305/mod-dlc-merger-merge-steam-gog-sod-dlc-or-custom-dlcs-with-the-main-game

 
General (Windows, Mac OS X, and Linux)
Make sure you back up your game acordingly, just to be sure. 

Extract the contents of the mod archive to your game's main directory. 
NOTE: For BG:EE it is important that you istall the mod to the language version you are playing the game in. Otherwise, the dialogues of the mod will not show but give error messages.


Windows
On successful extraction, there should be an bst folder and a setup-bst.exe file in your game folder. To install, simply double-click setup-bst.exe and follow the instructions on screen.
Run setup-bst.exe in your game folder also to uninstall the mod.

Mac OS X
If properly extracted, you should have a bst folder, setup-bst, and setup-bst.command in your game's main directory folder. To install, simply double-click setup-bst.command and follow the instructions on screen.
Also run setup-bst.command in your game folder to uninstall the mod.

Linux
Download the latest version of WeiDU for Linux from WeiDU.org and copy WeiDU and WeInstall to /usr/bin. Following that, open a terminal, cd to your BG installation directory.
Run WeInstall setup-bst in your game folder to install the mod. Then run wine BGMain.exe and start playing.


NOTE: BG(II):EE are actively supported games. Please note that every patch update will wipe your current mod setup! If in the middle of a modded game you might want to delay the patch update as even after reinstalling the mods, you might not be able to continue with your old savegames.

COMPATIBILITY

BS Balduran's Seatower Mod is coded using WeiDU and does not overwrite any files. It should be compatible with most other (WeiDU) mods. 

BP-Worldmap-Mod:
-The Seatower Mod is independent of the Worldmap Mod, but compatible. The new area will be patched to the Worldmap of all compatible games (BGT, BG:EE, EET). If the Worldmap Mod is needed for other mods, the preferred order would be BST - Worldmap Mod.
If the Worldmap Mod is installed after BST, the icon of the BST area will be also added but will be shifted on the worldmap.
